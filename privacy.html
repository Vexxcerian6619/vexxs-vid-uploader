Privacy Policy — Vexxs vid uploader

Last updated: October 6, 2026.

Data we collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we receive an OAuth access token and refresh token, your TikTok open ID, and basic profile information (such as your username). We also temporarily handle the video files you choose to upload.

How we use it

Your tokens and account identifiers are used solely to upload your videos to your own TikTok account at your direction. Videos are transmitted directly to TikTok's servers.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok's official APIs as required to perform uploads you requested.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored securely and used only for the upload function. Access tokens expire after 24 hours; refresh tokens are kept only to maintain the connection you authorized and are deleted if you revoke access or request deletion.

Your rights

You may revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings, which stops all future uploads. Contact the developer to request deletion of stored tokens.
